How the planner decides

1. Priority score for each subject: difficulty × (6 − confidence) × (1 + 10 ÷ days left). Harder subjects, weaker confidence and closer exams all raise the score.

2. Daily allocation: each day, the study hours are split among subjects whose exam is still ahead, in proportion to their score. That day's weight also rises as the exam gets closer. Time is given in 30-minute blocks using the largest-remainder method so the total always equals your daily hours.

3. Session type: the first 60% of a subject's days are Learn, the next 25% are Practice, and the last 15% (at least the final day) are Revise.

This is a rule-based, weighted scoring model: it is transparent and every number can be explained, which makes it easy to defend in a demo.
